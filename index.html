Microgravity Design Safeguards

  • Flyback Diode Protection: 1N4001 diodes installed across inductive motor loads to prevent ESP32 micro-resets from voltage spikes.
  • Backflow Prevention: Inline check valves and non-porous silicone tubing ensure fluid retention during microgravity pressure shifts.
  • Collapsible Reservoir: 1L bladder prevents bubble vacuum formation without needing buoyancy-assisted intake.
  • Targeted Airflow Pollination: Dual-zone canopy manifold agitates pollen directly, removing mechanical vibration failure points.
