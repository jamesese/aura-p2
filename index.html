Microgravity Design Safeguards

  • Flyback Diodes: 1N4001 diodes installed across motor loads to prevent ESP32 resets.
  • Check Valves: Inline valves & silicone tubing prevent fluid backflow in zero-g.
  • Collapsible Bladder: 1L water bag prevents vacuum formation without buoyancy.
  • Targeted Air Bursts: Canopy air manifold agitates pollen without fragile mechanical arms.
